## Related open-world research: NPC leash/reset
A separate research question concerns NPC reset distance and chase duration. A Forever-focused addon named Leash Helper Forever reportedly uses **level-dependent timer estimates**: 1–29: 11 s; 30–39: 12 s; 40–44: 13 s; 45–49: 14 s; 50+: 15 s. These are **unverified third-party estimates**, not server constants. No authoritative universal reset distance was established. Distinguish spawn-relative distance, time since threat interaction, crowd control, social aggro, and special NPC boundaries. Do not hard-code leash values into WildQuest routing without live verification.
Reference: https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/leash-helper-forever
