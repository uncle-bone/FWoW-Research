# World of Warcraft: Forever — live-launch intelligence
**Research date:** 2026-11-05. **Scope:** Public source review only; no local client or gameplay verification in this update.

## Evidence tiers
- OFFICIAL ANNOUNCED: Blizzard-authored announcement; not proof of observed gameplay.
- COMMUNITY DATABASE: Third-party structured data; check record provenance and current build.
- COMMUNITY REPORTED: Secondary press/player report; not independently verified.
- BUILD TRACKER REPORTED: External client build observation; not locally scanned.
- LIVE VERIFIED: Actual observed game/client evidence (none newly collected in this report).
