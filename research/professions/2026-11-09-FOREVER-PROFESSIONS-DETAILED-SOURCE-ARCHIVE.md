## Alchemy — factual source extraction

Source: https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/alchemy-leveling-guide
- Source's training brackets: Apprentice 1–55, Journeyman 55–150, Expert 150–225, Artisan 225–300.
- Apprentice has no character-level requirement in Forever; Journeyman requires level 10 / Alchemy 50; Expert level 20 / Alchemy 125; Artisan level 35 / Alchemy 200.
- Horde starting-area examples: Miao'zan (Sen'jin), Carolai Anise (Brill); Thunder Bluff for Mulgore. Alliance examples: Alchemist Mallory (Elwynn), Cyndra Kindwhisper (Dolanaar); Ironforge for Dun Morogh. Skyborne: Iaadaria Bitterwind, Valiena Swiftgale, Nyassa Swiftdraught (Zephras Isle).
- Journeyman capital trainers include Yelmak, Doctor Marsh, Bena Winterhoof, Lilyssia Nightbreeze, Tally Berryfizz, Sylvanna Forestmoon.
- Expert: Doctor Herbert Halsey (Undercity), Ainethil (Darnassus). Artisan: Rogvar (Stonard), Kylanna Windwhisper (Feathermoon Stronghold).
- Recommended 1–225 sequence features Minor Arcane Elixir or Elixir of Minor Force, Elixir of Minor Defense, Minor Mana Potion, Elixir of Minor Fortitude, Elixir of Wisdom, Lesser Mana Potion, Fire Oil, Elixir of Fire Power, Mana Potion and Nature Protection Potion. Alternatives are important for auction-house prices.
- 225–300 is explicitly marked untested; 290–300 route undecided. Healing potions shifted to First Aid, so Classic recipes should not be assumed.
- Reputation/merchant alternatives: Zandalar Tribe vendor Rin'wosho; Merchant's Favor vendors Apothecary Durelle (Horde, Crossroads vicinity) and Nina Surefire (Alliance, Three Corners, Redridge). Major Frenzy Potion is presented as a 290–300 option.
- Material totals are *estimates*, not exact deterministic crafts, because yellow skill-up recipes are probabilistic.

## Blacksmithing — factual source extraction

Source: https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/blacksmithing-leveling-guide
- Guide's leveling sections: Apprentice 1–75, Journeyman 75–150, Expert 150–215, Artisan 215–300, with specializations between Expert and Artisan.
- Shopping list is split into 1–150, 150–225 and 225–300. The final list is explicitly provisional; Thorium totals include bars reserved for four quests.
- The source recommends pairing with Mining and says craft counts are estimates; recipe alternatives replace the associated shopping-list row.
- Trainer coverage includes Horde and Alliance capitals, race starting zones and Skyborne's Zephras Isle. Darnassus has no trainer; Night Elves are directed to Auberdine. Undead Blacksmithing requires traveling to a trainer and buying a hammer in advance.
- Early workflow includes turning Rough Stone into sharpening/grinding stones, reserving some for later crafts, then using Copper and Bronze recipes. The guide explicitly says to keep 26 Rough Grinding Stones after the early step and all 36 Coarse Grinding Stones from skill 50–67 for later steps. This is a high-value carry-forward dependency for any step tracker.
- Journeyman recipe options compare vendor sale value and disenchanting yields, and call out vendor-sold Weak/Strong Flux.
- Do not turn its estimated materials into exact guaranteed output without testing skill-up probabilities in the target build.

## Enchanting — factual source extraction

Source: https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/enchanting-leveling-guide
- Guide sections: Apprentice 1–70, Journeyman 70–150, Expert 150–225 and Artisan 225–300. The page splits shopping materials at 225 and says level 35 is needed to progress beyond 225.
- Foreword says Forever added low-level recipes that make the first 70 points cheaper than Classic; pairing with Tailoring enables disenchanting crafted gear.
- The source says Apprentice has no level requirement; Journeyman requires level 10 and skill 50. Trainers are listed by capital and starting zone, including Skyborne trainers.
- Disenchant is learned with Apprentice Enchanting and is accessed through the profession window's Enchanting box, per the guide.
- It provides a two-line macro for crafting and confirming replacement enchants; macro/API availability is source-specific and must be checked against the installed Forever build before addon integration.
- Motes of Magic and rods have profession-vendor/Blacksmith acquisition instructions; Greater essences can be broken into three lesser essences in the guide's example.
- As with other routes, material counts are estimates and alternatives should be price-compared.

## Gathering and secondary-profession implications

- Mining's guide explicitly includes smelting as well as gathering, ore tracking, skill bonuses and zone/ore progression. Keep smelting and node-mining as distinct step types.
- Herbalism includes minimap tracking and route options; preserve all listed alternatives rather than selecting a single zone by default.
- Skinning's guide is organized around required skill and mob-level progression; it is not a conventional shopping-list craft guide.
- Fishing and Cooking are offered both jointly and separately. The combined version coordinates caught fish, recipes, training quests, and later zone steps; keep that dependency graph distinct from standalone guides.
- First Aid must remain a first-class profession route. The Alchemy article states some healing recipes moved into First Aid in Forever, so Classic profession assumptions can be wrong.
- Camping and Merchant's Favor are system guides, not professions themselves; they add camp crafting, profession workstations, faction reputation, recipe access, and cosmetic/mount rewards. Full item/reward names, exact turn-in quantities, camp locations and recipe tables still require a detailed fact-by-fact pass and live validation.
