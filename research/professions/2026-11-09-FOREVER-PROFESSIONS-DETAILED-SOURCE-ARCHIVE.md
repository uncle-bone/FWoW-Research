## Alchemy — factual source extraction
Source: https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/alchemy-leveling-guide
- Source's training brackets: Apprentice 1–55, Journeyman 55–150, Expert 150–225, Artisan 225–300.
- Apprentice has no character-level requirement in Forever; Journeyman requires level 10 / Alchemy 50; Expert level 20 / Alchemy 125; Artisan level 35 / Alchemy 200.
- Horde starting-area examples: Miao'zan (Sen'jin), Carolai Anise (Brill); Thunder Bluff for Mulgore. Alliance examples: Alchemist Mallory (Elwynn), Cyndra Kindwhisper (Dolanaar); Ironforge for Dun Morogh. Skyborne: Iaadaria Bitterwind, Valiena Swiftgale, Nyassa Swiftdraught (Zephras Isle).
- Journeyman capital trainers include Yelmak, Doctor Marsh, Bena Winterhoof, Lilyssia Nightbreeze, Tally Berryfizz, Sylvanna Forestmoon.
- Expert: Doctor Herbert Halsey (Undercity), Ainethil (Darnassus). Artisan: Rogvar (Stonard), Kylanna Windwhisper (Feathermoon Stronghold).
- Recommended 1–225 sequence features Minor Arcane Elixir or Elixir of Minor Force, Elixir of Minor Defense, Minor Mana Potion, Elixir of Minor Fortitude, Elixir of Wisdom, Lesser Mana Potion, Fire Oil, Elixir of Fire Power, Mana Potion and Nature Protection Potion. Alternatives are important for auction-house prices.
- 225–300 is explicitly marked untested; 290–300 route undecided. Healing potions shifted to First Aid, so Classic recipes should not be assumed.
- Reputation/merchant alternatives: Zandalar Tribe vendor Rin'wosho; Merchant's Favor vendors Apothecary Durelle (Horde, Crossroads vicinity) and Nina Surefire (Alliance, Three Corners, Redridge). Major Frenzy Potion is presented as a 290–300 option.
- Material totals are *estimates*, not exact deterministic crafts, because yellow skill-up recipes are probabilistic.

## Herbalism — factual source extraction
Source: https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/herbalism-leveling-guide
- Source brackets: 1–70, 70–115, 115–170, 170–205, 205–230, 230–270, 270–300. Each bracket has route maps on the original page; this archive does not reproduce those images.
- Horde capital trainers: Jandi (Orgrimmar), Martha Alliestar (Undercity), Komin Winterhoof (Thunder Bluff).
- Alliance capital trainers: Tannysa (Stormwind), Reyna Stonebranch (Ironforge), Firodren Mooncaller (Darnassus).
- Early trainers include Zor'la/Mishiki (Durotar), Garan Sunstrider (Mulgore), Florence Nightshade/Faruza (Tirisfal), Nancy Songflower/Herbalist Pomeroy (Elwynn), Emrys Flintbeard (Dun Morogh), Kali Healtouch (Loch Modan), Eleyna Duskbreeze/Malorne Bladeleaf (Teldrassil).
- Skyborne: early quest **Harvesting Windstones** can award **Wild Harvest**, teaching Herbalism; later island trainers Syriel Nightrain (Valanaar) and Halassa Fernbreeze (Shen'dar Village).
- The site links a separate article on new Forever herbs, seeds and camping items. This is a further research dependency.
- Route design for WildQuest should retain **all** valid zone alternatives at a given skill, not force a single map. Exact map shapes/coordinates require independent, permission-safe reconstruction or client validation.

These are separate Forever-specific systems that interact with professions. Research should capture camp item/buff requirements and the supply-crate reputation progression and recipe vendors; neither system should be reduced to a standard 1–300 trainer table. The linked pages have not been exhaustively transcribed or independently verified in this archive.

## Research database design
Store original, independently checked factual records with: `profession`, `skill_start`, `skill_end`, `zone`, `faction`, `trainer`, `recipe_or_node`, `material_ids`, `quantities_estimated`, `required_level`, `source_url`, `source_section`, `client_build`, `verification_status`, `alternatives`. Separate source claims from live observations. Do not fabricate item IDs, NPC IDs, map coordinates, or universal route superiority.
