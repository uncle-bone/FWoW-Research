Paladin Judgement of Command counts as melee/on-hit. Priest Quick and the Dead reduces Divine Spirit mana correctly. Shaman Windfury Totem activates on eligible main-hand melee abilities. - **Nov 16:** Druid Wild Growth rank cooldowns and Genesis/Pounce bleed fixed. Priest Dark Inspiration stacking fixed; Vampiric Embrace changes at scheduled weekly maintenance: 10% group healing (was 20%) and caster receives 100% increased healing, intended unchanged solo outcome. Rogue poison rank ceiling and Shank/combo-point behavior fixed. Shaman Windfury Totem armor/internal cooldown/Rage and proc eligibility fixed; Maelstrom Weapon double-cast and Thundering Strikes weapon requirement fixed. Warrior Unbridled Wrath, Flurry, Reckless Swings interactions with Cleave/Heroic Strike fixed; cooldown manager updated.

## Dungeons, XP and encounters
- Nov 9: dungeon mob XP increased **50% relative to the then-current rate**, not to 150% of Classic; the precise Classic-relative total is not officially established by this hotfix. https://bindonforever.com/patch-notes
- Nov 13: dungeon kill XP nullified for party when enemy trivial to a higher-level member. Nov 14 follow-up clarified/relaxed to **more than 12 levels above the enemy**; preexisting instances may retain previous rules until reset. https://theforeverera.com/en/builds ; https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-13-2026
- Deadmines Sneed eject fix, Drowned City non-boss respawn two hours (was one), Stratholme Balnazzar Legacy credit corrected.
- Nov 16 Drowned City Captain Dreadrise stun immunity removed, Braxton combat behavior fixed, Abyssal Bubble cannot block/dodge/parry. Sunken Temple Atal'alarion statue interaction corrected. https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-16-2026
- **Dreadwatch impact:** review mechanic observations for Drowned City and Sneed, and avoid treating boss immunity/respawn values from 70790 as current.

## Professions, Camping, items, economy
- Nov 16 Fishing Rack now grants lures to everyone; Bloated Fish proc drop chance increased and loot tables revised.
- Formula: Enchant Boots - Speed from reputation changed to bind-on-pickup.
- Zephras Isle cooking supplier Nyalah Brightfire expanded inventory.
- 70938 client index includes 31 profession records changed; source does not alone establish each recipe's live server behavior.
- Nov 10 most level-48+ quests gained additional gold rewards, except several categories (valuable item rewards, repeatables, trivial/easy quests). Not an XP-to-gold conversion at level 60.
- Auction House minimum deposit reduction 1 silver→1 copper and mail timing were initially announced, then temporarily rolled back, scheduled for weekly maintenance Nov 17/18 by region. **Verify live rollout by region before labeling active.**
- Vendor sell values for certain bind-on-pickup crafted armor reduced (Leatherworking, Tailoring, Blacksmithing).
